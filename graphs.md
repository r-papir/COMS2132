<ins>Defintions</ins>
+ **Graph:** a pair of two sets fo vertices and edges, **G = (V,E)**.
+ **Vertices:** the set of vertices or *nodes*
+ **Edges:** the set of edges; each edge is a pair **(v,w)**, where **v,w ∈ V**

V = {v<sub>1</sub>, v<sub>2</sub>, v<sub>3</sub>, v<sub>4</sub>, v<sub>5</sub>, v<sub>6</sub>}

E = {(v<sub>1</sub>, v<sub>2</sub>), (v<sub>1</sub>, v<sub>3</sub>), (v<sub>2</sub>, v<sub>3</sub> ),(v<sub>2</sub>, v<sub>5</sub> ),(v<sub>3</sub>, v<sub>4</sub>), (v<sub>3</sub>, v<sub>6</sub>),(v<sub>4</sub>, v<sub>5</sub>), (v<sub>4</sub>, v<sub>6</sub>), (v<sub>5</sub>, v<sub>6</sub>)}

Graphs may be **directed** or **undirected**.
+ In *directed* graphs, the edge pairs are ordered.
+ Edges often have some weight or cost associated with them (*weighted graphs*).

**Vertex *w*** is adjacent to **vertex *v*** if **(w,v) ∈ E**. A **path** is a sequence of vertices w<sub>1</sub>, w<sub>2</sub>, ...w<sub>k</sub>, such that **(w<sub>i</sub>, w<sub>i+1</sub>) ∈ E**.
